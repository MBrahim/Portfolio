# Supercapacitors Backup Controller
A high-reliability energy backup controller designed to manage supercapacitor-based power systems, ensuring uninterrupted supply during main power loss while providing controlled charging, voltage balancing, and system protection for embedded and industrial applications.

## Features
### Energy Storage Management
- Designed for supercapacitor bank integration with controlled charge and discharge operation.
- Active monitoring of capacitor voltage levels to prevent overvoltage conditions.
- Support for multi-cell series configurations with balancing considerations.

### Power Path Control
- Automatic switchover between main supply and supercapacitor backup during power failure.
- Smooth transition mechanism to prevent output voltage drops.
- Optimized power routing for efficiency and reliability.

### Charging Control
- Controlled inrush current during supercapacitor charging phase.
- Protection against reverse current flow.

### Voltage Regulation
- Stable regulated output for downstream electronics during backup mode.

### Protection
- Overvoltage protection (OVP).
- Undervoltage protection (UVP).
- Overcurrent and short-circuit protection.
- Reverse polarity protection on input stage.

### Indicators & Monitoring
- Status LEDs for power presence and backup mode indication.
- Test points for voltage monitoring and diagnostics.
- Clearly labeled connectors for supercapacitor bank, input supply, and regulated output.

## Applications
- Embedded Systems Backup Power
- Industrial Controllers
- Robotics & Automation Platforms
- Data Retention & Safe Shutdown Systems

This controller provides a robust and efficient solution for supercapacitor-based backup systems, enabling seamless power transition, controlled charging, and comprehensive protection to ensure system stability during unexpected power interruptions.
